Privacy Policy

Anime Figure Shop · Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy explains what the Anime Figure Shop app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. You can browse figures without an account; we only collect what is listed below once you sign in.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Orders: what you order, your phone number, delivery address (for delivery orders) and any note you add.
  • Shop owners: your shop's name and phone number, your stock records, and the customers you add (name, phone, address, note).
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends the error, your phone model and system version. It does not include your name, email or orders.

Why

  • To take your order, contact you about it, and deliver it.
  • To let shop owners keep their stock and customer records.
  • To find and fix crashes.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

Who else sees it

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics), which processes it on our behalf. Only you, the shop handling your order, and the app's administrator can see your orders. A shop owner's stock and customers are visible only to that shop owner and the app's administrator.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete it, your account is removed, pending orders are cancelled, and your phone number, address and note are erased from your past orders. The orders themselves (items and totals) are kept for the shop's sales records.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete my account and enter your password. Shop owners: your account holds your shop's records, so contact us below and we will close your shop and delete your account.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
